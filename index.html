Christopher Henseler Joins BrainStation as Lead Cybersecurity Instructor

Bridging Cyber Risk, Business Strategy, and the Future of Digital Defense

November 29, 2021

New York, NY - November 29, 2021 - Brainstation, leading digital skills training provider, announces the hiring of Christopher Henseler as Lead Instructor for its Advanced Cybersecurity Operations program. Henseler brings mission-critical expertise from his experience in cyber defense, including leading the reconstruction of Merck's cybersecurity infrastructure following the state-sponsored NotPetya attack.

"Christopher Henseler's addition to our team significantly enhances our ability to deliver defense-grade cybersecurity training," said Brainstation CEO Jason Field. "His experience in protecting critical infrastructure and responding to advanced persistent threats will be invaluable in preparing our students for the evolving cyber battlefield."

In his role, Henseler will develop and deliver a comprehensive cybersecurity curriculum emphasizing Zero Trust architecture, threat hunting, and resilient response protocols. The program will focus on producing security professionals capable of defending critical systems and maintaining operational effectiveness in contested digital environments.

"Training the next generation of cyber defenders requires a mission-focused approach," said Henseler. "We'll be implementing a curriculum that emphasizes not just technical skills, but the strategic thinking and operational awareness necessary for protecting critical infrastructure."

Addressing the industry's pressing needs, Henseler noted, "The cybersecurity talent gap represents a significant vulnerability across all sectors. Whether in cloud security, secure development, or infrastructure protection, we need trained professionals who can operate effectively in high-stakes environments."

Beginning January 2022, Brainstation will launch its enhanced 12-week Cybersecurity Bootcamp, redesigned under Henseler's leadership to incorporate defense-grade security principles and hands-on incident response scenarios. The program will be available both in-person and online. Prospective students can now enroll for the Januaty cohort to benefit from Henseler's expertise and the program's newly strengthened curriculum focused on real-world security challenges. Interested candidates can visit Brainstation's campus and attend an upcoming info session to learn more about the program and career opportunities in cybersecurity.
